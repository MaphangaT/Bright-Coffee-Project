Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Project


**Business Performance**

🔍 Key Executive Insights
Core Revenue Comes from Routine Behavior
Weekdays contribute the largest share of revenue
Predictable demand allows for efficient planning
👉 Bright Coffee is operating as a routine-based business
   Midweek Is the Revenue Engine
Wednesday and Thursday show the most stable and highest returns
👉 These days should be treated as critical profit drivers
   Weekend Opportunity Is Underutilized
Strong performance on Saturday
Noticeable drop on Sunday
👉 Indicates untapped revenue potential, especially on Sundays
    Imbalance in Daily Performance
Clear gap between high-performing days (midweek, Saturday) and low-performing days (Sunday, early week)
👉 Suggests opportunities for demand balancing strategies
💡 Strategic Recommendations
Maximize Peak Days (Midweek):
Ensure optimal staffing, speed of service, and product availability
Grow Sunday Revenue:
Introduce targeted promotions, brunch offerings, or loyalty incentives
Leverage Saturday Strength:
Upsell premium products and bundle deals to increase average transaction value
Stabilize Low Days (Monday & Sunday):
Use discounts, marketing campaigns, or partnerships to boost traffic.
